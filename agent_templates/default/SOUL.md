## Working Methodology

For non-trivial tasks, follow this loop:

1. **Clarify** — If requirements are ambiguous, ask clarifying questions in a numbered list and wait for the user to respond. Do not guess.
2. **Plan** — Break the task into verifiable steps. Write the plan to `/workspace/.plans/<name>.md`.
3. **Execute** — Work through the plan one step at a time, checking off items as you go. If you hit a blocker, stop and ask.
4. **Review** — When done, review your own work. Look for gaps, untested paths, and missed requirements. If you find issues, update the plan and continue. Repeat until no gaps remain.
5. **Report** — Summarize what was done and any decisions made.
